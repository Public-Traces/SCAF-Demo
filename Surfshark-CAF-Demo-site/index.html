    System Overview
    
        
      
          
    1. 1
      User types on a keyboard
      
            
      When text fields on selected apps are active, a desktop app locally stores short chains of one-way, collision resistant logs of key presses on selected apps, conditional on a hardware signal that the press originates from trusted default hardware
      2. 
          
    2. 2
      User completes writing session
      
            
      When the typing session is complete, the laptop compares and signs the hash chains with a secret key stored on the device’s security chip. This happens completely in the background, invisible to users.
      3. 
          
    3. 3
      User sends off the text
      
            
      Tweets, emails, posts, blogs. Wherever a user shares their text on the open Web, the signature of that text invisibly travels with it.
      4. 
          
    4. 4
      Anyone can verify
      
            
      A public verification portal lets any recipient or reader of text verify the hidden signature.
      5. 
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Improving transparency and authenticity on a free Web
    
        
    
          
    
            
    What our demo can do now
    
            
      
              
    • Demonstrate text was physically typed on the author's laptop keyboard, or conversely that it comes from a third-party or agentic source
      • 
              
    • Rejects keystrokes injected by agentic software without relying on complex and adversarial behavioral classification models.
      • 
              
    • Demonstrates the magic honesty that comes with recognizing the human quality of content on the internet
      • 
              
    • Applies privacy-enhancing techniques to verify text without tracking or storing messages online.
      • 
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    How the Advocacy Fund can help
    
            
      
              
    • Improving the hardware attestation through additional certificates and work on Apple’s developer suite
      • 
              
    • A fleshed out onboarding experience, and further work to minimize device permissions and software complexity.
      • 
              
    • Experiment with playful, thoughtfully designed hardware which builds trust and improves human trust on an increasingly agentic Internet.
